Five websites, one model

Designed and built by Claude Opus 5.

Five fictional companies, each with its own brand, copy, layout and interaction design. The last two build their products in 3D in the browser. Every page is a single HTML file.

01

Morrow Salt Works

Sea salt harvested at low tide. The hero is cut into layers, and scrolling floods and drains the salt pans over 24 hours of real tide.

02

Nightjar Sleeper Co.

European night trains. A split-flap departures board, and a train window drawn in code that carries you through the night, stopping at every station.

03

Overprint

A risograph studio. Pick any two of twelve inks and the whole site reprints, photos included. A poster prints one pass at a time as you scroll.

04

Verre 50/1.2

A camera lens modelled in 3D. It opens, separates into ten glass elements, and traces light through them to a single point on the sensor.

05

Fieldnote K65

A keyboard in 3D you can actually type on. It comes apart into seven layers as you read, and repaints in three colourways.
