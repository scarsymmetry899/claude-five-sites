Wake up already there.

An hour before arrival your attendant knocks with coffee and a warm roll. The blinds go up on a city that's only just starting.

Centre to centre

Every Nightjar arrives at a main station in the middle of the city. No transfer, no taxi queue.

Showers on arrival

Berth and cabin guests get a free shower and a locker at the arrival station until noon.

Half the carbon

A night train uses a fraction of the energy of the same trip by air. We publish the figures for every route each year.
