Case

Six degrees, and no feet to fold out.

The case is milled from one block of aluminium with the typing angle built in, so it never wobbles or creeps across the desk. It weighs 1.6 kg, and a third of that is a brass weight you’ll never see.

Inside

Seven layers, each one there for the sound.

    Hot-swap

    Change a switch in ten seconds.

    Every socket takes a switch without soldering. Pull one out with the tool in the box and push in another. Try all three of ours, or any switch with three or five pins.

    Gasket mount

    It gives a little under your fingers.

    The plate floats on eight rubber gaskets instead of being screwed to the case. Each press flexes it a fraction of a millimetre, which is where the soft, even sound comes from.

    Colourways

    Three colourways, all anodised twice.

    Every keycap is thick PBT plastic with dye-sublimated legends, so the letters won’t wear off or turn shiny, even after years of use.
